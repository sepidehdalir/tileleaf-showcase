Tileleaf: Poster Printing · Support

Check scale before assembly

Select the paper size loaded in your printer. Print at 100% / Actual Size with Fit and Shrink disabled. Measure both outside edges of the guide square: each must be 50 mm. Printer paths, including AirPrint, can apply scaling. If the square is wrong, correct the print settings or use a PDF viewer with actual-size controls before printing the artwork.

Trim and join

Cut the outer dashed artwork boundaries. On interior tiles, trim the left and top overlap at the inner dashed lines. Match the repeated artwork, assemble each row, then join the rows. Save the guide and job code for replacement sheets.

Sources and sharpness

Choose a file up to 40 MB, an image up to 80 MP/30,000 pixels per side, or an unencrypted PDF up to 100 pages. One selected page is imported at a time. Images and PDF pages are normalized to a maximum 4,096-pixel edge; PDF pages are flattened and transparency becomes white. Large enlargements from small sources can look soft.

Purchases and local projects

Complete exports up to four artwork tiles are free. Lifetime Pro adds larger bounded layouts, saved projects and selected-tile reprints. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that bought Pro. An entitlement change does not delete saved projects. Deletion removes the app's local source/layout copy; PDFs shared elsewhere are separate.

Contact

Email hello@arvinify.com with your device/iOS version, paper size, print application and the step that failed. For scale questions, include the measured square and your selected settings. Avoid attaching private artwork unless you intend to share it.
