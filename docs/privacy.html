TILELEAF / POSTER PRINTING

Your artwork stays yours.

Privacy policy · Updated October 19, 2026

Tileleaf: Poster Printing is made by Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir. The app processes artwork on your device. We do not collect your artwork, imported files, project contents, advertising identifiers or usage analytics through the app. No account is required.

Images, PDFs and projects

You choose individual images or documents through Apple's native pickers. A Files provider may download your selected document according to that provider's own service. Tileleaf normalizes a local image copy for the poster workflow; it does not upload the source to our servers.

Saved local projects keep an app-owned image copy and layout information in protected app storage. They may be included in system device backups. You can delete projects within the app. Removing the app removes its local projects; copies in device backups are managed separately through Apple's backup settings.

Exports and printing

When you choose Share, Save or Print, the generated PDF goes to the destination or printer you select. That destination may have its own privacy practices. Copies you save or send elsewhere remain there until you remove them. Temporary export files are cleaned after a day on the next app launch.

Purchases

Apple handles Lifetime Pro purchases and payment details. Tileleaf reads Apple's verified purchase entitlement to unlock Pro. We do not receive your payment card information. Apple may provide aggregate store sales and analytics reports under its own policies.

No tracking services

Tileleaf has no advertising, analytics, tracking or external AI SDK, no backend account, and no automatic content sharing. The app uses file metadata only to bound selected imports and manage its own local files.

Support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your address and support question. Please do not send private artwork unless you intend to share it for support. Contact hello@arvinify.com or visit arvinify.com.

We update this page when the app's data practices change.
